GRID4EARTH is an ESA Digital Twin Earth activity building a unified, standardized data infrastructure based on Discrete Global Grid Systems (DGGS)—HEALPix and Zarr—for Copernicus Sentinel and Destination Earth data.

**Project site** → [eopf-dggs.github.io](https://eopf-dggs.github.io/)
