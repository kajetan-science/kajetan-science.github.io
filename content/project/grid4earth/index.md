GRID4EARTH is an European Space Agency Digital Twin Earth project building a unified, standardised data infrastructure based on Discrete Global Grid System (DGGS) for Copernicus Sentinel and Destination Earth data.

By leveraging DGGS and cloud-optimised formats like Zarr, data from different missions can be integrated more consistently and efficiently. This reduces the complexity of data processing and supports numerous use cases, from environmental and climate research to operational decision-making across Earth system service domains.
