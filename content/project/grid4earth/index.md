GRID4EARTH is an European Space Agency Digital Twin Earth project building a unified, standardised data infrastructure based on Discrete Global Grid System (DGGS) for Copernicus Sentinel and Destination Earth data.

**Project site** → [eopf-dggs.github.io](https://eopf-dggs.github.io/)

**Read more** → [A bit of (bit) magic](/water/musings/bit-of-bit-magic/) — why DGGS indexing exists and how the same operation that wins a children's-game lets a million Earth-observation cells fit into 8 MB of RAM.
