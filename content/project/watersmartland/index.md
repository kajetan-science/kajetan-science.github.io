WaterSmartLand is an ERC Consolidator Grant developing a framework to find spatially optimal land management for nature-based solutions (wetlands, riparian buffer strips) to reduce agricultural nutrient runoff. It uses a DGGS data cube, machine learning, and process-based hydrological modelling.

**Project site** → [water-smart-land.eu](https://water-smart-land.eu/)

<!--more-->

Led by Prof. Evelyn Uuemaa at the University of Tartu (2024–2029).
